Who I Am

Hi, I'm Sujal Achari — also known as Suz41 on GitHub. I'm based in India.

I'm not a traditional developer. I build things by combining AI tools with my own ideas, turning thoughts into working web apps. No frameworks, no build steps — just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

My main interest is movies and film. I've built tools to create cinematic banners, log personal movie reviews, and track what I watch — all running offline in the browser with no cloud or accounts needed.
